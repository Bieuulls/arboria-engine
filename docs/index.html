01 / THE VISION

Not another
empty benchmark.

What if advanced geometry rendering could be developed in the open—where contributors inspect the algorithms, reproduce the measurements, and improve the system together?

ARBORIA explores clustered meshes, hierarchical detail, GPU visibility, indirect rendering and resource-aware streaming. We are building on top of an open game-engine ecosystem instead of hiding behind unexplained performance claims.

We are not claiming to be the world's best engine today. We are inviting the community to help us build something worthy of that ambition.
